Jindal Supreme (India) Limited, incorporated in March 1974 and based in Hisar, Haryana, manufactures steel pipes, tubes, and structural steel products for infrastructure, construction, water supply, oil & gas, and rural electrification applications. Its product portfolio includes MS black pipes and tubes, galvanized pipes, metal beam crash barriers (W-beam and Thrie-beam, launched FY2025), and GI tubular poles (launched FY2026). The company serves B2B customers — infrastructure contractors, institutional buyers — through a dealer network of 53 dealers across northern India.. India's steel pipe and tube sector is tied to infrastructure capex — roads, railways, housing, oil & gas pipelines. Government's National Infrastructure Pipeline, PM GatiShakti, and the 2026 budget's emphasis on capital expenditure (₹11.11 lakh Cr outlay) support steady demand. However, commodity price volatility (HRC steel prices), Chinese import competition, and rural demand cycles make revenues lumpy. The sector outlook is stable-to-positive on infrastructure tailwinds.. National Infrastructure Pipeline and PM GatiShakti programme directly drive demand for steel pipes and structural products used in roads, railways, and utility infrastructure. The 2026 Union Budget maintained capex at ₹11.11 lakh Cr, with focus on roads, railways, and urban infrastructure. Post-issue P/E of 14.33x is cheaper than listed India steel-pipe and TMT peers — compare Behari Lal Engineering (17.21x), Rajputana Stainless (21.10x), VMS TMT (22.24x); at 3.87x P/BV the issue is attractively valued on book, though PAT margin compression (−7% YoY) is a concern. Hero Motors Limited, incorporated in April 1998 and headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, is an automotive technology company designing, developing, and manufacturing engineered powertrain solutions for global automotive OEMs. Its two segments are Powertrain Solutions (Gears & Transmissions, and Bike Powertrain — the latter a first-mover in global e-bike powertrains and the only Indian manufacturer exporting CVT hubs and integrated electric powertrain products for e-bikes) and Alloys & Metallics (sheet metal and tubular assemblies for automotive OEMs). Customers include BMW, Ducati, enviolo, Formula Motorsport, Hummingbird EV, and HWA.. India's auto-component sector is gaining from the EV transition — government's FAME II scheme (though winding down), PLI for auto components, and state EV policies are driving adoption. Global OEMs are sourcing EV drivetrain components from India. But traditional ICE component volumes face a long-term plateau. EV powertrain pure-plays command premium valuations, but execution risk is high — technology shifts, customer concentration, and cyclical auto demand all bite.. PLI scheme for auto components and advanced chemistry cell (ACC) battery storage, plus state-level EV policies (e.g., UP, Maharashtra) directly support EV powertrain manufacturing. FAME II subsidies (being phased out) previously boosted EV two-wheeler adoption. Post-issue P/E of 92.31x is significantly richer than listed India auto-component peers — compare Dhoot Transmission (41.36x), Tenneco Clean Air India (28.97x), SEDEMAC Mechatronics (126.91x); Hero Motors sits in the expensive half of the peer range, pricing in aggressive EV growth expectations. National Stock Exchange of India Limited operates India's largest stock exchange — a vertically integrated multi-asset platform spanning trading, clearing, settlement, listing, market data, and index services across equities, derivatives, currency, commodities, and debt. NSE holds a near-monopoly in Indian equity derivatives (99.48% options, 74.71% futures by turnover) and 99.79% cash market share. The exchange served 129 million unique investors and 3,005 listed entities with ₹474 trillion market cap as of June 2026.. India's capital markets are in a structural bull run — market-cap-to-GDP hit ~119%, retail participation surged post-COVID, and daily turnover on NSE regularly exceeds $5-7 billion. Exchange revenue scales with turnover, listings, and derivatives activity, all trending up. Government push for financialisation of savings and the SEBI-regulated market structure reinforce exchange moats.. Post-issue P/E of 35.40x compares against global exchange peers (e.g., CME Group ~25-30x forward). No direct listed Indian exchange peer — NSE is effectively a monopoly. Premium to global peers reflects India's higher-growth profile. GVT and technical products account for 74% of FY26 revenue. India's ceramic tile sector is consolidating around organised players as real estate and infrastructure construction picks up. Morbi cluster accounts for ~70% of India's tile production. Pooja Logistics Ltd., incorporated in 2011 and headquartered in New Delhi, provides temperature-controlled ("cold chain") road transport for perishable goods across India through an owned fleet of over 424 GPS-enabled refrigerated trucks as of March 2026. It serves confectionery and bakery, dairy, quick-service restaurant, pharmaceutical, and e-commerce clients who need continuous cold-chain integrity from pickup to delivery. India's cold chain logistics market is expanding on rising frozen-food consumption, pharma cold-chain compliance requirements, and e-commerce/quick-commerce grocery delivery growth. The sector remains fragmented, with Snowman Logistics the only major listed pure-play and most capacity still owned by small regional operators — consolidation and organised-player expansion are the near-term themes.
