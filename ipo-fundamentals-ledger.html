National Stock Exchange of India Limited operates India's largest stock exchange, providing trading, clearing, and settlement services across equity, derivatives, debt, and currency segments. The IPO is an offer-for-sale (OFS) of 21.3 crore shares by the Government of India, reducing the state's stake from 55.26% to 26.01%. NSE is the world's largest exchange by number of trades and second-largest by market capitalisation traded. The business is a near-monopoly inIndian equity derivatives and enjoys deep competitive moats from technology infrastructure, brand trust, and network effects. The lead manager for this issue is Kotak Mahindra Capital Co.Ltd..

Bottom Line

Three high-GMP issues (SS Retail at 34%, Jindal Supreme at 29%, and to a lesser extent Hero Motors at 6.55%) await listing on 23 September 2026. NSE remains the most fundamentally attractive open issue given its monopoly exchange business and 58.8% PAT margin, though the OFS-only structure means no fresh capital raise. Sonaselection India and Varmora Granito are early-stage stories with modest GMP — monitor subscription trends. All assessments based on public data; not personalised advice.
