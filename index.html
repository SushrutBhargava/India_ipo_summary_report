National Stock Exchange of India Limited operates India's largest stock exchange — a vertically integrated multi-asset platform spanning trading, clearing, settlement, listing, market data, and index services across equities, derivatives, currency, commodities, and debt. NSE holds a near-monopoly in Indian equity derivatives (99.48% options, 74.71% futures by turnover) and 99.79% cash market share. The exchange served 129 million unique investors and 3,005 listed entities with ₹474 trillion market cap as of June 2026. India's market-cap-to-GDP ratio of 119% (vs. 224% US, 199% Canada) signals substantial headroom for exchange revenue growth. The IPO is a 100% offer-for-sale of 12.64 crore shares by sovereign and institutional selling shareholders; no fresh capital is raised — proceeds go entirely to sellers. Listing on BSE only. Post-issue P/E of 35.4x compares against global exchange peers (e.g., Chicago Mercantile Exchange trades at ~25–30x forward earnings), suggesting NSE is priced at a premium to international comparables, consistent with India's higher-growth profile but leaving limited margin for listing-day re-rating. The lead manager bench is led by Kotak Mahindra Capital Co.Ltd. — a Strong bench with an extensive 2026 mainboard mandate book and a track record of several issued issues listing with gains.

Sonaselection India Limited is an integrated fabric manufacturing and processing company based in Bhilwara, Rajasthan. It manufactures 100% cotton fabric, cotton lycra (stretch) fabric, cotton blends, and polyester blends, and processes greige fabric on a job-work basis for third-party exporters. The company operates a 49,540 sq. m. facility with 82.44 million metres per annum processing capacity, equipped for bleaching, dyeing, and finishing. Founded in 2022, it has grown revenue 64% YoY to ₹517.60 Cr, driven by the transition from a job-work model to in-house manufacturing after acquiring a processing unit in 2022 and commissioning a new plant in July 2024. The IPO is a fresh issue of 1.43 crore shares (no OFS) — proceeds will repay ₹80 Cr of borrowings, fund ₹33.57 Cr of capex for plant and machinery expansion, and cover general corporate purposes. Post-issue P/E of 16.53x sits broadly in line with listed Indian textile-processing peers such as Aastha Spintex (18.78x) and Alpine Texworld (12.68x), placing the issue at a fair entry point within its peer band. The lead manager, Choice Capital Advisors Pvt.Ltd., is an Unproven bench — no 2026 mainboard track record was found on chittorgarh's performance tracker or ipomarkets.com, making this a debut mandate for the firm.

It runs under proprietary brands 'SS Mobile', 'Mobile Exchange Wala', and 'The Mobile Space' through COCO, COFO, and FOFO formats with local franchisee partnerships. The company is the largest mobile phone retail chain in West India and Maharashtra, and the third largest in India among its peers. FY26 revenue grew 47% to ₹2,352.85 Cr with PAT up 49% to ₹59.28 Cr. The IPO is a fresh issue of 85.08 lakh shares (₹360 Cr) plus an OFS of 33.02 lakh shares (₹140 Cr) by promoters and a non-promoter shareholder; proceeds fund working capital (₹241.35 Cr), store fit-outs (₹12.45 Cr), and general corporate purposes. Bottom Line

Three listings this week (23 Sep): SS Retail and Jindal Supreme carry the strongest listing-day pop signals — 34.2% and 29.0% GMP respectively, backed by 107x and 177x subscription. Both are worth watching for early selling pressure given full valuations. Hero Motors lists the same day but its 7x subscription and 92x P/E warrant caution — the EV theme may not carry the stock if QIB demand was thin.

Open issues: NSE is the highest-quality business in the cohort (monopoly exchange, 51% PAT margin, zero debt) but its OFS-only structure offers no fresh capital raise — the ₹22,562 Cr raises money for sellers, not the company. Sonaselection India is the best-valued open issue at 16.53x post-issue P/E but needs to show subscription momentum before 21 Sep; the unproven lead manager adds execution uncertainty.

Upcoming: Varmora Granito opens 22 Sep — strong PAT growth and a strong lead-manager bench are positives, but 60.66x P/E for a low-margin tiles business leaves little room for disappointment.
