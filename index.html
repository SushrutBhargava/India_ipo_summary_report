The four-question filter this ledger scores against

01 · FINANCIAL HEALTH

Revenue & PAT growth

Growing consistently across the three disclosed years, without debt or cash flow that looks unstable.

02 · VALUATION

P/E and P/B

Weighed against listed peers where the prospectus discloses a comparable set — no peer set is invented where none is disclosed.

03 · FUND USAGE

Where proceeds go

Fresh proceeds going to expansion or working capital reads constructively; heavy debt repayment or a pure share sale (OFS) is a caution flag.

04 · SUBSCRIPTION

QIB and NII demand

Institutional cover in the closing hours of bidding, where the book has opened — large institutional demand signals conviction from large investors.
