The Four-Question Filter

Q1

Financial Health

ROE, ROCE, D/E, PAT margins — is the business fundamentally sound?

Q2

Valuation

P/E, P/BV weighed against listed peers — but only where the prospectus itself discloses a comparable set. No peer set is invented where none is disclosed; the multiple is then read on its own, not against a fabricated comparison.

Q3

Fund Usage

Growth capex, debt repayment, or OFS — where does the money go?

Q4

Subscription

Overall & category multiples — does the market agree?
