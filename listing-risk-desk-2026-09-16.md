# IPO Listing-Day Risk Desk — 16 September 2026

**Scope:** every issue in the IPO Fundamentals Ledger currently "Closed — awaiting listing" (14 issues) plus every mainboard/SME name that listed in the last 5 trading days (16, 15, 14, 11, 10 Sep 2026 — 6 issues). 20 issues tracked below.

**Nature of this document:** advisory research only. No brokerage order of any kind was placed, simulated, or queued as part of producing this report. Every recommendation line is monitoring/exit-timing language for a human to act on themselves through their own broker — never an instruction this system executes.

**Methodology notes (read before using the numbers):**
- GMP is the unofficial, unregulated grey-market premium sourced from investorgain.com/chittorgarh.com, refreshed the morning of 16 Sep 2026 — it moves daily and is not a regulated price signal.
- Subscription multiples (QIB/NII/Retail/Overall) are from chittorgarh.com's live BSE+NSE combined subscription report, most recent print as of compile time; "closed, final" means bidding has ended and the figure is the confirmed final book.
- **Anchor Unlock Ratio** = anchor investor allocation value (₹Cr) ÷ total issue size (₹Cr), used here as a disclosed-data proxy for "anchor share of the newly listed free float" — precise post-listing free-float share counts are not uniformly disclosed pre-listing, so this ratio is the most defensible number obtainable from anchor-allocation circulars without fabricating a free-float denominator. This is a documented judgment call, not the literal SEBI free-float definition — treat the Low/Medium/High band as directional, not exact.
- Lock-in dates: SEBI ICDR mandates 50% of anchor shares unlock 30 days after allotment, the remaining 50% at 90 days. Where a company's own circular disclosed exact unlock dates, those are used; where only the anchor bid date was found, the 30-day/90-day dates are computed from that bid date and flagged "(computed, not individually disclosed)."
- Risk score (0–100, Low 0–33 / Medium 34–66 / High 67–100) is a simple weighted heuristic across four factors, each scored 0–25: GMP momentum, subscription skew (institutional vs retail imbalance), sector volatility (qualitative sector read), anchor-unlock proximity (days to nearest unlock date). It is not a validated statistical model — treat it as a structured read of the same public data everywhere else in this report, not a black-box signal.
- No number below is fabricated. Every field marked "pending" means no source disclosed it as of compile time.
