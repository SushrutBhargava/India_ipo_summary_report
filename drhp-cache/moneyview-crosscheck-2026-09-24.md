The other anchors were Motilal Oswal (two schemes, 4,861,584 and 1,902,915 shares), 360 ONE Equity Opportunity Fund (Series 2: 3,749,568; Series 4: 1,470,735), Amundi Funds Asia Equity Focus (2,941,470), HDFC Retirement Fund – Equity Plan (2,941,029), HDFC Life (2,942,793) and three smaller ABSL Apex long-short schemes (588,294, 294,147 and 294,147).

News reports give different investor counts. PTI, carried by Rediff, says "20 funds" and matches the 20 line items. Moneycontrol says "12 anchor investors", which appears to count fund houses rather than schemes. Inc42 and Moneycontrol give "9.63 crore" shares. That is a rounded version of 96,324,729.

**Lock-in.** RHP p.122 [PDF 127], "Lock-in of Equity Shares Allotted to Anchor Investors": 50% of the anchor shares are locked in for 90 days from the date of Allotment, and the other 50% for 30 days. The price band ad gives the expected Basis of Allotment date as "On or about Tuesday, September 29, 2026". Adding 30 and 90 calendar days to that date gives **October 29, 2026** (30-day) and **December 28, 2026** (90-day). I computed these dates myself; no source has published them yet. Chittorgarh's anchor report (https://www.chittorgarh.com/report/mainboard-ipos-anchor-investors/163/) did not list Moneyview when checked on September 24, 2026. The Moneyview detail page (https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/moneyview-ipo/2936/) had no anchor section either. If the actual allotment date differs, both dates move with it.

**Anchor Unlock Ratio.**

- Post-issue shares: 1,76,02,31,268 at the upper end of the band (price band ad, shareholding table; also shown on Chittorgarh as "Share Holding Post Issue").
- Promoter and Promoter Group holding after the offer, at the upper end: 33,98,43,776 shares. This is the sum of the 11 rows in the price band ad's shareholding table. Promoters: Puneet Agarwal 11,97,26,975; Sanjay Aggarwal 14,54,54,525; Sushma Abburi 86,21,676. Promoter Group: Chitra Agarwal 80,64,600; Payal Himatsingka 20,00,000; Agarwal Family Trust 4,70,00,000; Ravindra Abburi 23,25,000; Sandeep Agrawal 23,25,000; Sarita 23,25,000; Tvaya Trust 1,000; Ashwin P Gramopadhye 20,00,000. The table assumes full subscription. The final numbers will be in the Prospectus.
- Free float used: 1,76,02,31,268 − 33,98,43,776 = **1,42,03,87,492 shares** (19.31% promoter-group share, computed).
- Each tranche is 50% of 96,324,729 = 48,162,364.5 shares. The fraction is left in because the RHP does not say how each investor's odd share is rounded.

| Unlock date (computed) | Anchor shares unlocking | ÷ free float 1,42,03,87,492 | ÷ total post-issue 1,76,02,31,268 |
|---|---|---|---|
| October 29, 2026 (30-day) | 48,162,364.5 | 3.39% | 2.74% |
| December 28, 2026 (90-day) | 48,162,364.5 | 3.39% | 2.74% |
| Cumulative | 96,324,729 | 6.78% | 5.47% |

The ratios are rounded to two decimals; the inputs are exact.

Caveat on free float: "free float" here means total post-issue shares minus the promoter group, as the brief specified. It is not the number of shares tradable on listing day. Pre-IPO shareholders who are not promoters, such as Accel, Tiger (Internet Fund III), Ribbit and Apis, are still inside this figure, and SEBI ICDR rules lock in their pre-offer shares for 6 months from allotment. The float actually tradable in October to December 2026 is smaller, so the real unlock ratio is higher than shown above. I did not compute that tighter float, because the RHP does not disclose the lock-in exemptions (for example, shares held for 1 year or more by VCF/AIF/FVCI) in a form I could sum exactly.

### 3. DLG (default loss guarantee) exposure

**DLG cap.** RHP Risk Factor 5, p.31 [PDF 36]: the company provides "a DLG of up to 5% of the loan portfolio" under the RBI DL Directions. "Our Business", p.136 [PDF 141], adds that the cap is "a maximum of 5% of the loan portfolio on disbursement value". The cover is posted as fixed deposits with a lien marked in favour of the regulated entity (RE), or as bank guarantees.

**DLG book.** RHP "Objects of the Offer", p.136 [PDF 141], table of DLG arrangements. Figures are ₹ million.

Sources: Risk Factor 5, p.32 [PDF 37], and Objects, p.137 [PDF 142]. The RHP describes DLG outstanding as "the maximum contractual exposure of the Company under the DLG arrangements … being the maximum loss share that may become payable". Per Objects p.137, DLG Disbursals grew 17.63% in FY26. ₹3,250.00 million of the Net Proceeds will go towards raising DLG outstanding: ₹1,250.00 million in FY27 and ₹2,000.00 million in FY28.

**DLG portfolio size (principal outstanding of the loans covered by DLG): not disclosed.** I searched the RHP for "DLG portfolio", "covered under DLG", "DLG … AUM" and off-book or partner AUM. The RHP gives DLG *disbursals* and DLG *outstanding* (the maximum exposure) but no figure for the outstanding principal of the DLG-covered loan book. I have not derived it from Managed AUM minus WFPL Portfolio Loans, because the RHP does not present that subtraction.
