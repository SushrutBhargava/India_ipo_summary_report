**Prior-year figures were restated between the DRHP and the RHP.** FY25 PAT rose by 57.68 lakh (computed), mostly because FY25 current tax was cut from 622.72 to 563.36. The RHP (p241, notes C.4.8 and D) explains that the short/(excess) tax provision was "restated and reclassified to the respective years". Its net-worth reconciliation shows a restatement "Change in Profit/(Loss)" of (45.30) in FY26, 126.18 in FY25 and (25.88) in FY24. FY25 RoNW moved from 44.81% to 62.11%: the new figure appears to be on average net worth, but the RHP does not state the basis.
