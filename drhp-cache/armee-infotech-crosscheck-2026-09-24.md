## Offer (price band ad, Sep 18, 2026, plus corrigendum, Sep 22, 2026)

- Price band ₹350 to ₹375, face value ₹10, lot 40. The issue is all fresh, with no OFS.
- **P/E on FY26 diluted EPS (₹19.16): 18.27x at the lower end and 19.57x at the upper end.** The ad gives an industry peer average of 70.63x.
- Weighted average RoNW over three years: 31.42%.
- At cap: 80,00,000 shares, ₹ 30,000.00 lakh. Post-issue market cap: 3,17,31,386 shares = ₹ 1,18,992.69 lakh (original ad).
- Floor figures in the original ad were 85,71,400 shares, ₹ 29,999.90 lakh and a market cap of 3,23,02,786 shares = ₹ 1,13,059.75 lakh. **The corrigendum replaced the floor row with 85,71,428 shares = ₹ 30,000.00 lakh (fresh issue and total issue size).** I could not read the corrigendum's market-cap row reliably: the OCR is garbled and a vision re-read was capped. My arithmetic gives 3,23,02,814 shares × ₹350 = ₹ 1,13,059.849 lakh at floor. This is unconfirmed.
- WACA of secondary transactions: ₹25.00. Jayshree Kamal Patel acquired 5,40,000 shares from Kiritkumar Patel. Floor = 14 times and cap = 15 times WACA. There were no primary transactions in three years.
- Anchor (letter, Sep 22, 2026): 10,65,000 shares at ₹375 = ₹ 399,375,000. NIL to domestic mutual funds and NIL to insurance or pension funds.

### Exceptional / one-time items

- There are no exceptional items in any period. The RHP's "Profit before exceptional items and tax" equals PBT (RHP p.377 [PDF 382]).
- **FY26 other income was 1,346.72 lakh, against 247.12 in FY25.** The MD&A (RHP [PDF 376]) says this was "primarily due to an increase in interest income of ₹ 11,157.88 lakhs earned on account of delayed payment received from a customer". **That quoted amount is larger than total other income, so the RHP's own text is internally inconsistent**, probably a typo. The delayed-payment interest is one-off. It supplied 1,346.72 of the 6,099.45 lakh PBT (share not stated in the RHP).
- Finance costs rose to 2,494.11 lakh from 672.45. Total debt rose 3.6 times in one year, from 4,810.12 to 17,436.07 lakh (my calculation: 3.62 times).
- IT Managed Services revenue fell after the e-Gram Vishwa Gram manpower contract was largely discontinued from February 2026, a year before its scheduled end.
