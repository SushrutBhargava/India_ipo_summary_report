- Revenue growth (revenue from ops) = 2,220.93 / 2,101.28 − 1 = **+5.69%**

## Key risk items

- Geographic concentration: West Bengal store = 90.72% of FY26 revenue from operations
- Customer concentration rising: top 10 customers = 67.32% of FY26 revenue vs 57.68% in FY25
- Brand concentration: "large part of revenues" dependent on top five brands (RF3, not independently quantified with a %age in the sections reviewed)
- Negative operating cash flow in FY24 (-₹43.12 lakh) and FY26 (-₹65.66 lakh), driven by rising inventory (+₹201.52 lakh FY24, +₹429.60 lakh FY26)
- Promoter's average cost of acquisition (₹5.89/share) is 9.3x below the ₹55 issue price
- Promoters/directors have given personal guarantees on company borrowings
- Belated FLA/ODI-Part-III filing with RBI (submitted 8 Aug 2025) for deregistered Hong Kong subsidiary Denn Global Ltd — a technical FEMA non-compliance, not a live liability
- No long-term customer contracts; low repeat-purchase rate flagged as a risk factor; single-godown inventory concentration
