### Exceptional / one-time items

Exceptional items are "–" in all years. The auditor's "other matter" note (p258) says FY24 items (interest on fixed deposits and GST DRC-03 payments for earlier indirect-tax liabilities) that had been taken directly to reserves were re-routed through the P&L on restatement; no amount is given there. Other income includes income from write-offs and a security deposit received against a labour licence that had previously been expensed (p315), but amounts were not extracted.
