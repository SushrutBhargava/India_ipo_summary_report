Reconciliation: FY25 loss before tax (1,205.88) = loss before exceptional items (469.83) + exceptional items (736.05). On the before-exceptional line, the loss narrowed 20.07% in FY26 (375.55 vs 469.83, computed). The narrowing in the reported loss (455.06 vs 1,263.06) is mostly the absence of FY25's exceptional items.

**Other one-off item in FY26:** "Liabilities no longer required written back" of 157.60 (cash-flow statement p78; in other income, which rose from 117.51 to 272.84). Excluding it, the FY26 loss before exceptional items and tax would be (533.15) (computed), which is wider than FY25's (469.83).
