## Bonus share issue (material for per-share comparability)
RHP Capital Structure section (Notes to Statement of Shares, p.~275-276) discloses: "Add: Bonus Equity Shares allotted during the year 75,05,271" taking the share count from 8,33,919 to 83,39,190 during FY26. This means FY25's NAV of ₹244.76/share and FY26's NAV of ₹39.17/share are **not directly comparable** on a per-share basis without adjusting for the ~9:1 bonus ratio (83,39,190 / 8,33,919 ≈ 10.0x, i.e. roughly a 9-new-for-1-old bonus). A separate note (p.~183) also records an earlier, smaller bonus issue of 7,72,500 shares in a 60:1 ratio during FY2022-23 — an unrelated, much earlier capital event.

## Related-party / land-title risk on the funded project (RHP Risk Factor 17, p.~46)
"Our Company, through one of its Group Entities Aurika Developers LLP, is engaged in a real estate development project situated at Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. The land on which the said project is being developed is not owned by our Company or Aurika Developers LLP but is held in the name of Advika Buildtech LLP, one of our Group Entities. Although Aurika Developers LLP holds 44.92% partnership in Advika Buildtech LLP..." — the ₹26.00 Cr of net proceeds funds a project on land the ultimate recipient entity holds only a 44.92% indirect stake in, via a further Group Entity layer.

## Key risk items (RHP Internal Risk Factors, Section II)
- RF1: revenue concentration — top-5 customers 67.69% of FY26 revenue (down from 71.87% FY25, but up from a lower 87.53% FY24 base which itself reflects an even smaller, newer business); no long-term contracts, project-specific mandates only
- RF2: geographic concentration in Uttar Pradesh and Haryana (Noida, Gurugram)
- RF5: does not own registered/corporate office premises; premises shared with multiple Group Entities/subsidiary under separate leases
- RF6: litigation — 1 criminal proceeding against the company (unascertainable amount); 1 civil litigation against directors/promoters (unascertainable amount)
- RF7: UP RERA real-estate-agent registration has expired; renewal application pending
- RF17: Ayodhya project land held by a further Group Entity (Advika Buildtech LLP) in which the direct recipient of issue proceeds (Aurika Developers LLP) holds only a 44.92% partnership interest
