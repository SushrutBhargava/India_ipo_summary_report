## Offer

- Price band ₹60 to ₹64 (chittorgarh; anchor letter confirms allocation at ₹64). Lot 2,000 shares; individual minimum 4,000 shares (₹2,56,000).
- Shares: 80,67,500 pre-issue, up to 1,10,67,500 post-issue (RHP pdfp45).
- Issue at cap: 30,00,000 × ₹64 = ₹19,20,00,000 (computed; chittorgarh body: ₹19.20 crore).
- Anchor: 8,32,000 shares allocated at ₹64 on Sep 23, 2026 (anchor letter) = ₹5,32,48,000 (computed; chittorgarh: ₹5.32 crore). Investor-wise lines are illegible in the scan.
- RHP reservation (pdfp45): QIB not more than 13,90,000 (of which anchor up to 8,32,000); NII not less than 4,20,000 (1,40,000 for 2 lots to ₹10 lakh; 2,80,000 above ₹10 lakh); individual investors not less than 9,80,000.
