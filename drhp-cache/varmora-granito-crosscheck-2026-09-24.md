### Exceptional / one-time items

- FY26: ₹7.74 mn extra gratuity provision under the New Labour Codes, effective November 21, 2025 (MD&A, RHP p.411 [PDF 415]). There are no exceptional items in FY25 or FY24.
- Profit before exceptional items and tax rose 109.28% in FY26, from 363.93 to 761.65 (the RHP gives the same figure). Reported group PAT rose 79.03% (computed). Revenue from operations rose 4.59%.
- Non-controlling interests matter here. Minority losses of ₹69.64 mn in FY26 mean owners' profit (620.57) is higher than group PAT (550.93). EPS and RoNW are computed on owners' profit.
- FY25 depreciation was 1,194.08, against 616.11 in FY24 and 1,059.87 in FY26. This drove the FY25 profit dip. It is not tagged as exceptional.
