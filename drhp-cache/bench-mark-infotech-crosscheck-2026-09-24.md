### One-time items in the profit

There is no exceptional line in the P&L, but the RHP's own other-income annexure (p226, Annexure XXXI) classifies **"Creditors written back" of 293.47 (FY26), 25.33 (FY25) and 17.96 (FY24) as "Non-Recurring and not related to Business Activity"**. In FY26, other income is 25.41% of PBT (RHP figure).
