**Price band ad vs RHP conflict.** The BS Delhi ad (OCR) states the fresh issue as **₹ 12,850.00 lakh** (73,42,857 shares at ₹175; 69,45,946 at ₹185). The RHP cover, the Objects section and the abridged prospectus all state **₹ 12,900.00 lakh**; the string "12,850" never appears in the RHP as an issue size. Share counts in the ad compute to ₹12,850 lakh (69,45,946 × 185 = 12,850.0001 lakh). Chittorgarh follows the ad (₹128.50 Cr). I did not find a corrigendum or addendum explaining the ₹50 lakh reduction.

### Exceptional / one-time items

- There are no exceptional items in any period (the line is blank in FY24 to FY26).
- The restatement adjustments table (RHP [PDF 304]) moves profit before exceptional items and tax from the audited 3,695.22 to the restated 3,701.97 in FY25 (-6.75), and from 1,844.04 to 1,882.95 in FY24 (-38.88).
- FY26 net worth doubled. This came from the Pre-IPO placement (₹4,000.00 lakh) plus FY26 PAT: equity capital rose from 2,475.00 to 2,717.42. FY26 RoNW of 35.44% is therefore computed on a post-placement equity base.
- Finance cost doubled to 1,689.86 from 817.96, while borrowings were flat.
- EBITDA definitions differ within the RHP. The ratios note deducts other income; the KPI note adds back finance costs excluding lease interest. This explains the 4,388.78 vs 4,388.76 difference for FY25.
