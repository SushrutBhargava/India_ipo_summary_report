## Key risk items (RHP Internal Risk Factors, Section II)
- RF1: outstanding litigation — against the company ₹141.62 lakh (GST/tax + civil); against promoters ₹1,468.00 lakh (large civil matter, relief sought is specific performance so amount for the promoters' own claim is unascertainable)
- RF2: manufacturing facility-II and warehouses on leased premises
- RF3: top-10-customer concentration jumped to 61.28% of FY26 revenue from 29.11% (FY25) and 27.04% (FY24) — a sharp YoY increase in concentration, not a stable level
- RF4: negative-cash-flow risk language, though all three disclosed years (FY24-FY26) show *positive* operating cash flow per the RHP's own KPI table (₹698.97 lakh FY24, ₹433.51 lakh FY25, ₹883.35 lakh FY26) — see Discrepancies
- RF5: delays in statutory dues payment
- RF7: history of delayed ROC filings (Form 23/32), delays ranging 11-284 days, concentrated in 2007-2010
- RF8: absence of formal agreement for business acquisitions of M/s Tirupati Plastic Industries, M/s Noble Polymers, M/s Shreeji Plastic Industries and M/s Balaji Polymers
