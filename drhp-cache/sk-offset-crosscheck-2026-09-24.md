## Offer (price band advertisement and filled Basis for Issue Price)

- Price band ₹119 to ₹125. Face value ₹10. The cap price is "12.50 TIMES OF THE FACE VALUE". Lot size is 1,000 shares (chittorgarh).
- Ad wording: "PRICE TO EARNING RATIO BASED ON DILUTED EPS FOR FISCAL 2026 AT THE FLOOR PRICE IS 8.51 TIMES AND AT THE CAP PRICE IS 8.93 TIMES."
- On weighted-average EPS of 8.24, the P/E is 14.44 at the floor and 15.17 at the cap (Basis for Issue Price).
- There is no industry P/E because the company says it has no listed peers.
- WACA for primary issuance is ₹120.25 after bonus adjustment. This comes from converting a loan into 1,05,930 shares at ₹481 on December 08, 2025 (RHP p.115). The cap price of ₹125 is only 1.04 times that WACA. The ad shows "1,04 Times", which is the OCR reading.
- The OCR text did not show a market-cap figure. My calculation, not an official figure: pre-issue 54,18,320 shares × ₹125 = ₹67,72,90,000; post-issue 77,43,320 × ₹125 = ₹96,79,15,000.
- Anchor book: ₹6.75 crore for 5,40,000 shares, bid on Sep 22, 2026 (chittorgarh). I did not pull the anchor letter.

### Exceptional and one-time items

- The P&L has no exceptional line.
- FY24 includes non-operating items. Other income was 177.85, of which "Liabilities written back (164.86)" appears in the cash flow, and there was a profit on sale of assets of 10.52. Without these, FY24 PBT of 99.46 would be much lower.
- Tax: FY24 includes "MAT Credits Entitled/Utilised 11.85". FY26 has a deferred tax credit of -55.96.
- RoNW is calculated on **year-end** net worth, not the average. This inflates the ratio in FY26: net worth tripled after the Dec-2025 loan conversion (509.52) and retained profit.
- Working capital: FY26 inventories rose from 573.34 to 2,394.45 (cash-flow line "(1,821.11)"). FY25 trade receivables rose by 2,360.08, which made FY25 operating cash flow negative at (1,079.67).
- FY26 PAT growth is 384% (chittorgarh), from 154.41 to 747.96. The FY26 PAT margin is 11.22%, against 3.20% in FY25 and 3.35% in FY24. The RHP gives no one-off reason for the margin jump. EBITDA margin rose from 11.65% to 21.27%.
