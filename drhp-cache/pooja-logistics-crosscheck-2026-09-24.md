## Offer (price band advertisement)

- Price band ₹109 to ₹115. Face value ₹10. Minimum bid 2,400 shares, then multiples of 1,200.
- "THE PRICE TO EARNING RATIO BASED ON DILUTED EPS FOR FISCAL 2026 AT THE FLOOR PRICE IS 9.08 TIMES AND AT THE CAP PRICE IS 9.58 TIMES."
- RHP p.104 also gives these P/E ratios:
  - on FY25 EPS: 9.89 (floor) / 10.45 (cap)
  - on FY24 EPS: 19.02 / 20.06
  - on weighted-average EPS: 10.25 / 10.81
- Peer P/E: highest 11.93, lowest 6.94, average 9.44.
- Market cap: not stated in the ad text I extracted. Computed from chittorgarh's post-issue shares: 1,42,84,000 × ₹115 = ₹1,64,26,60,000 (₹164.27 Cr per chittorgarh).
- Issue size: ₹44.23 crores (chittorgarh body text). 38,46,000 × ₹115 = ₹44,22,90,000.
- Anchor: ₹12.21 crore, 10,62,000 shares (chittorgarh). The anchor letter was not pulled.

### Exceptional and one-time items

- None on the face of the P&L ("Exceptional Items –" in all three years).
- The fall in FY26 RoNW from 54.20% to 36.21% comes from equity growth, not falling profit. PAT rose 11.95% (computed). Net worth rose from 2,584.81 to 4,230.51: reserves rose 1,601.90 and 411.72 of share capital was raised in FY26 (financing cash flow).
- FY26 operating cash flow was 319.13, down from 1,392.61. Other current assets rose 1,082.47 and trade receivables rose 747.18.

Growth FY26 over FY25 (computed): total income +11.48%, PAT +11.95%.
