## Derived (at upper band ₹118)

- Revenue growth = 9,973.96 / 8,752.97 − 1 = **+13.95%** (chittorgarh states "14%" rounded, and separately "51%" for PAT growth — both match)
- PAT growth = 772.01 / 512.59 − 1 = **+50.61%**
- PAT margin = 772.01 / 10,062.09 = **7.67%** (RHP's own KPI table states 7.67% on Total Income basis, consistent — no basis discrepancy here unlike some other cards)
- EBITDA margin = 1,018.95 / 9,973.96 = **10.22%** (matches RHP KPI table exactly)
- EPS pre (chittorgarh convention: FY26 PAT lakh × 100000 ÷ pre-issue shares 89,53,509) = **₹8.62**
- EPS post (FY26 PAT ÷ post-issue shares 1,21,83,909) = **₹6.34**
- P/E pre = 118 / 8.62 = **13.69x**
- P/E post = 118 / 6.34 = **18.61x**
- P/BV = 118 / 20.92 = **5.64x**

All five of these match chittorgarh's displayed IPO Valuation table exactly.

## Key risk items

- RF1: revenue concentration — top 1 customer 42.72% (FY26), top 10 customers 84.59% (FY26)
- RF2: PAT margin surge (3.77%→5.82%→7.67% over FY24-26) attributed to a shift toward higher-ticket international events (49 events ≥₹50 lakh in FY26 vs 31 in FY25); RHP itself flags this may not be sustainable
- RF9: promoter Ravi Rajak previously a director of Aadvik Traders Pvt Ltd, currently under voluntary strike-off by RoC
- RF18: 'EVENTIONS' trademark still registered in promoter Cristoo Arora's personal name; assignment deed executed 6 Mar 2026, registration pending
- Litigation: 6 tax proceedings against the company (₹83.03 lakh aggregate), 10 against promoters (₹188.11 lakh aggregate), 1 material civil litigation
- Delays in filing statutory forms with RoC for the Sep 2025 bonus and rights issue
