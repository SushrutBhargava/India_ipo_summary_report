Recomputing backwards, 4.29 × 34.59 implies an effective price of ~₹148, which matches none of the RHP's disclosed price points or share-count variants tested (pre-issue net worth/pre-issue shares, or an approximated post-issue net worth/post-issue shares). **Flagged as an unverified/unreconciled figure** rather than silently corrected — reported as chittorgarh states it in the card, with this caveat.

### RHP-disclosed peer range (Basis for Issue Price, p.96)
The RHP discloses only an **industry peer-group P/E range**, with no named individual peer companies (unlike Nityas' RHP, which names its peers):

| Highest | 351.44x |
| Lowest | 31.70x |

Pind's post-issue P/E is **below even the RHP's lowest disclosed peer P/E** (26.12x vs 31.70x), i.e. priced at a steep discount to its own peer range. Given the peer set is unnamed and the range is extremely wide (31.70x–351.44x), this is reported as a data point rather than a strong buy signal — such wide dispersion suggests limited comparability of the underlying peer set to a single-city restaurant/hospitality operator. No peer set was invented; the RHP genuinely discloses only this aggregate range, not individual peer names.

## Material risks found in RHP
1. **Third-party food delivery app dependency:** 78.38% of FY26 revenue (₹1,916.48 lakhs), 85.19% FY25, 86.40% FY24 — high and structurally significant aggregator dependency (RHP risk factor #1).
2. **Rising leverage + tightening liquidity:** D/E up from 0.71 to 0.88 (+24%); Current Ratio fell below 1.0 (0.90) — short-term liquidity is now tighter than a year ago, concurrent with the Haveli Project capex ramp.
3. **Declining returns for 3 straight years:** ROE 22.85%→20.92%→15.65%; ROCE ~16.18%(FY24, approx.)→19.64%→15.83% (non-monotonic but net decline over the period, with FY26 as the low point on both metrics).
4. **Supplier concentration:** top-10 suppliers were 93.52% of FY26 purchases (up from 82.59% FY24), including packing-material vendors.

None of these red flags are prominently surfaced on chittorgarh's summary page; all are pulled directly from the RHP's Risk Factors and Ratio Analysis sections.

## Overall verdict
**Watch.** The headline valuation looks attractive (26.12x post-issue P/E, below even the RHP's lowest disclosed peer P/E of 31.70x), but this coincides with an 11.33% PAT decline, rising leverage (D/E 0.71→0.88), a sub-1.0 current ratio, and a lead manager whose only other 2026 SME listing is negative. The discount to peers may reflect genuine fundamental softening rather than a market mispricing — worth tracking subscription data and Haveli Project execution before treating the low P/E as a clear buy signal.
