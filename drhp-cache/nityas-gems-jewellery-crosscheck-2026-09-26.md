- FY25: −₹100.52 million (~−₹10.05 Cr)
- FY24: −₹10.51 million

Driven by rapid inventory growth (₹51.35mn FY24 → ₹410.54mn FY26) and receivables growth (₹40.64mn → ₹160.64mn) as the business scaled. This is a genuine, material fact from the RHP's Restated Statement of Cash Flows that chittorgarh's summary page does not surface, and it materially qualifies the otherwise excellent P&L growth story (RoNW 43.75%, PAT +128%). Flagged as the primary "Watch" driver in the card.
