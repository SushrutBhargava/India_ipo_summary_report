### Exceptional and one-time items

- The restated P&L shows no exceptional items in any year: "Add/(Less):- Exceptional/Prior Period Items" is "-" for all three years.
- **Tax holiday (non-recurring).** Tax expense was 9.16 on PBT of 748.13 in FY26, and 1.65 on 224.83 in FY25. This is because the company claims the Section 80-IAC startup deduction: (759.70) in FY26 and (239.96) in FY25 (Annexure 27, p.273 [PDF 276]). MAT of 124.88 (FY26) and 37.42 (FY25) was booked and then reversed as MAT entitlement. The MAT credit closing balance is 162.30. Risk Factor 5 (p.30) says the holiday covers FY25, FY26 and FY27 only. At the 25.17% nominal rate shown in Annexure 27, FY26 tax would have been 188.29 instead of 9.16. I have not restated PAT, because the RHP gives no normalised figure.
- **Cash flow.** Operating cash flow was negative in all three years: (383.44), (139.11) and (55.10). The cause was growth in receivables (up 494.74 in FY26) and inventory (up 563.13 in FY26).

Growth, FY26 over FY25 (from the RHP MD&A, p.239): total income +105.37%, revenue from operations +105.12%. PAT growth, computed: (738.97 − 223.18) / 223.18 = +231.11%.
