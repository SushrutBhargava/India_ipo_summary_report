## Offer (chittorgarh; the RHP has no prices)

- Price band ₹74 to ₹78. Lot size 1,600 shares. The minimum individual application is 2 lots (3,200 shares), which costs ₹2,49,600.
- Issue size: ₹21.99 crores in the page body, "₹22 Cr" in the header. Fresh issue only; no OFS and no anchor portion.
- Shares: 76,67,805 before the issue and 1,04,87,005 after.
- Market cap at the cap price: ₹81.80 Cr. Check: 1,04,87,005 × ₹78 = ₹81,79,86,390.
- Official P/E from a price band ad: **pending**. RHP p.107 shows "[●]". Computed on the RHP FY26 basic and diluted EPS of ₹5.88: ₹74 / 5.88 = 12.5850x at the floor and ₹78 / 5.88 = 13.2653x at the cap. Industry average per the RHP: 18.90x.

### Exceptional and one-time items

- FY26 exceptional item: gratuity provision of 55.77. It appears on the P&L face (p.58) and in MD&A (p.261: 0.11% of total income). I found no narrative explaining it. Tax effect: not disclosed.
- Before the exceptional item, PBT grew from 416.06 to 525.50, which is +26.30% (computed). Reported PAT grew from 346.06 to 451.13, which is +30.36% (computed). The RHP does not give PAT before the exceptional item net of tax.
- FY24 PAT of 493.60 is higher than FY25 because the FY24 tax charge was 104.23 against 70.01 in FY25. Margins are thin. PAT margin on total revenue is 0.87% in FY26, 0.86% in FY25 and 1.34% in FY24. The business is staffing and payroll, where pass-through employee cost was 32,424.63 in FY26.
