### Exceptional and one-time items

- There are no exceptional items on the P&L in any period.
- Growth in FY26 is mostly acquired. The RHP p.136 footnote says: "Of ₹ 6,023.91 Lakhs of Revenue from Operations for Fiscal 2026, ~60% is attributable to the consolidation of revenue from our subsidiary, AdLift Marketing". Standalone repeat business in FY26 was 54.21%. The consolidated figure is 22.77%.
- Goodwill on consolidation of 2009.88 is 58.79% of FY26 net worth of 3,418.66 (my calculation).
- The FY24 RoNW of 294.89% is a small-base effect: net worth was 209.68 at FY24 and (80.62) at FY23.
- Prior-period tax adjustment: (11.27) in FY26, 11.08 in FY25, 43.74 in FY24.
- The FY26 debt figure depends on the definition. Borrowings are 979.30, but the KPI debt-equity ratio is 0.29.
