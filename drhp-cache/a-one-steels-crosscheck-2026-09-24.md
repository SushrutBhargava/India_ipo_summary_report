### Exceptional / one-time items

- **FY26: exceptional income of ₹52.68 lakh from an insurance claim received** (MD&A, RHP [PDF 592]).
- **FY25: exceptional expense of ₹443.69 lakh from a fire** at the Gauribidanur plant that damaged a furnace (PPE) (RHP [PDF 594]).
- There is a sign-convention trap in the P&L. It prints "Less: Exceptional items 52.68", yet PBT (16,702.47) is higher than pre-exceptional profit (16,649.79). The 52.68 is therefore added, i.e. it is income, consistent with the MD&A.
- FY25 was a trough: PAT 771.05, against 12,740.82 in FY26. Chittorgarh's "PAT rose 1552%" compares against this depressed base.
- Government grants of 1,875.84 lakh in FY26 (259.19 in FY25) are counted inside total revenue from operations for KPI purposes.
