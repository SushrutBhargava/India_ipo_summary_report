## Company profile

Incorporated 1994 (formerly Sejal Farms Private Ltd.), civil engineering / manufacturing / construction
company. Manufactures Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC) sleepers for railways, precast/prestressed concrete
products, and fabricates/erects MS pipes, liners and penstock pipes for Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs).
Also provides EPC services for railway infrastructure, irrigation and civil-engineering projects.
Operational units across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha
and Karnataka. 420 employees as of 30 Jun 2026.
