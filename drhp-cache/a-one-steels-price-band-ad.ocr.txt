2. Execution risk relating to our proposed expansion and infrastructure projects: We are currently undertaking certain expansion and infrastructure projects at our
Koppal Facility, including a 10 MW waste heat recovery boiler (“WHRB”) power plant, of which 6 MW has been partially operational as of the date of the Red Herring
Prospectus, a proposed 6,00,000 MTPAiron ore beneficiation plant and a proposed railway siding. The WHRB power plant is expected to be fully commissioned during
the second quarter of Fiscal 2027, the iron ore beneficiation plant is expected to be commissioned in phases during Fiscals 2027 and 2028, and the railway siding is
expected to become operational in June 2027. These projects involve significant capital expenditure, construction and implementation risks and regulatory
dependencies. Any delay, cost overrun, technical or operational difficulty, inability to obtain or maintain requisite approvals or failure to achieve the expected utilisation
levels, cost efficiencies or other anticipated benefits could adversely affect our business, financial condition, cash flows and growth prospects.

3. Related party transactions and potential conflicts of interest: We have entered into, and may continue to enter into, transactions with our related parties in the
ordinary course of business, including sale and purchase of goods, unsecured borrowings, lease-related transactions and personal guarantees. While such
transactions have been undertaken on an arm’s length basis and in compliance with applicable law, certain non-core related party transactions, including borrowings,
rental income and interest expenses, have historically involved significant values. Further, as of July 15, 2026, our Promoters and certain members of the Promoter
Group had provided unsecured loans amounting to =13,766.80 lakhs and personal guarantees aggregating to $6,28,584 lakhs in connection with certain borrowings of
our Company and Subsidiaries. Related party transactions may involve actual or potential conflicts of interest, and there can be no assurance that such transactions,
individually or in the aggregate, will always be in the best interests of our Company or our minority Shareholders or will not adversely affect our business, results of
operations, cash flows and financial condition.

8. Volatility in steel demand and prices of key raw material: The steel industry is cyclical and steel prices are subject to fluctuations based on, among other factors,
domestic and international demand and supply, availability and cost of raw materials, imports, transportation costs, trade measures and conditions in key end-use
industries such as construction and infrastructure. Our cost of raw materials consumed amounted to %3,48,694.57 lakhs, %3,05,372.91 lakhs and %3,39,451.31 lakhs in
Fiscals 2026, 2025 and 2024, respectively, representing 84.05%, 86.22% and 88.53% of our Revenue from Operations. Further, our three largest manufactured
product categories, namely TMT bars, pipes and tubes and sponge iron, collectively contributed $2,55,613.29 lakhs, $2,39,900.60 lakhs and %2,33,249.49 lakhs in
Fiscals 2026, 2025 and 2024, respectively, representing 61.61%, 67.73% and 60.83% of our Revenue from Operations. Any decline in steel prices or demand,
particularly where not accompanied by a corresponding reduction in raw material and other input costs, may compress our margins and adversely affect our business,
results of operations, cash flows and financial condition.

9. Dependence on performance of key end-use industries: Our business is dependent on demand from the key industries in which our customers operate, including
construction and infrastructure and other industrial sectors. Demand in such industries is subject to economic cyclicality and may be affected by, among other factors,
changes in government policies, commodity prices, infrastructure spending, availability of substitute materials and general macroeconomic conditions. Further, our
three largest manufactured product categories, namely TMT bars, pipes and tubes and sponge iron, together contributed 61.61%, 67.73% and 60.83% of our Revenue
from Operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025 and 2024, respectively. Accordingly, any sustained slowdown in the end-use industries consuming these products or reduction in
demand from customers operating in such industries could adversely affect our sales, revenue, profitability, cash flows and financial condition.

10. Regulatory, statutory and environmental compliance risks: We have been subject to past and ongoing proceedings and actions by regulatory and statutory
authorities. In June 2019, SEBI imposed penalties of 7.00 lakhs and 9.00 lakhs on entities which now form part of our Company in relation to alleged manipulative

the Registrar of Companies and/or the Regional Director levied penalties against our Company, certain Directors and Promoters for violations of provisions of the
Companies Act, 2013, which have been paid and the relevant matters stand closed. We are also involved in ongoing proceedings before the Karnataka Electricity
Regulatory Commission in relation to alleged non-compliance with Renewable Purchase Obligations for Fiscal 2018. Further, between 2022 and 2025, our Company
received two notices in relation to the Hindupur Facility, ten notices in relation to Bellary Facility |, three notices in relation to the Gauribidanur Facility and eight notices in

Red Herring Prospectus, no further action has been initiated against our Company pursuant to such notices. Separately, our Subsidiary, Vanya Steels Private Limited,
was directed to close its operations pursuant to an order of the Pollution Control Board in relation to alleged environmental non-compliances. The Karnataka High Court
has stayed such closure order and the matter is currently pending. Any adverse determination in existing or future regulatory, statutory or environmental proceedings
may result in monetary penalties, additional compliance obligations, remedial measures, restrictions on or suspension of our operations, additional costs or
reputational harm and could adversely affect our business, results of operations, cash flows and financial condition.
