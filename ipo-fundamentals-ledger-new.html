Bottom Line

Three listings this week (23 Sep): SS Retail and Jindal Supreme carry the strongest listing-day pop signals — 34.67% and 31.18% GMP respectively, backed by 107x and 177x subscription. Both are worth watching for early selling pressure given full valuations. Hero Motors lists the same day but its negative −2.38% GMP, 7x subscription, and 92x P/E warrant caution — the EV theme may not carry the stock if QIB demand was thin and grey-market sentiment has turned bearish.

Open issues: NSE is the highest-quality business in the cohort (monopoly exchange, 51% PAT margin, zero debt) but its OFS-only structure offers no fresh capital raise — the ₹22,562 Cr raises money for sellers, not the company. NSE's GMP has corrected sharply (₹142 → ₹61) over two sessions, suggesting the grey market is reassessing the rich ₹1,700–1,785 price. Sonaselection India is the best-valued open issue at 16.53x post-issue P/E but needs to show subscription momentum before 21 Sep; the unproven lead manager and elevated 2.48 D/E add execution risk. BSE-only subscription at 0.2179x is thin.

Upcoming: Varmora Granito opens 22 Sep — strong PAT growth (79%) and a strong lead-manager bench (JM Financial + Goldman Sachs + SBI Capital) are positives, but 60.66x P/E for a low-margin tiles business leaves little room for disappointment. Pooja Logistics (BSE SME, ₹44.23 Cr) opens 23 Sep — too early for a view without the DRHP.
