## Method

- **Score** = 0.30 × GMP momentum + 0.25 × subscription skew + 0.20 × sector volatility + 0.25 × anchor-unlock proximity. Each component is 0-100.
- **GMP momentum** = 0.6 × min(100, GMP% × 1.25) + 0.4 × fade from the investorgain high (a large GMP or a GMP that has fallen from its peak both add listing-day risk).
- **Subscription skew** = 100 × retail multiple ÷ (retail + QIB multiple); +20 when the overall book is under 5x. Retail-heavy books score higher.
- **Sector volatility** is a fixed judgement per sector, stated per issue below.
- **Anchor unlock ratio** = anchor shares unlocking at each date ÷ free float at listing. Free float here is the total shares offered in the IPO, since pre-IPO non-promoter holders are under a 6-month lock-in. Each issue unlocks half its anchor book at 30 days and half at 90 days (chittorgarh 'Shares Locked' rows). Component = min(100, 30-day ratio × 3). Bands: Low <15%, Medium 15-30%, High >30%.
- **Anomaly flag**: overall >75x, or any category >100x, or GMP >50% of issue price.
- **Recommendation**: a fast exit is suggested only when the score is High or an anomaly flag fired; otherwise hold and monitor.
